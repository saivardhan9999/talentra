# Step 12 — Data Classification and Privacy (classification register)

Generated by `python v2/phase2/tools/build_phase2.py` from the v2 model (`db/migrations`) and the curated notes in `tools/notes.py`. Edit those, then rerun; do not edit this file by hand.

Conforms to schema [S-12](schemas.md#s-12--data-classification-and-privacy). Classes are the docket's (rule group 6): Public, Internal, Personal, Sensitive. The playbook's "confidential" maps to Sensitive.

## 1. Classification rule

1. **Sensitive:** credentials, session tokens, device identifiers, tax identifiers, payment gateway references (docket list).
2. **Personal:** every non-key attribute of an entity whose row is about one identifiable person (user, candidate profile and its sections, resume, consent, device, session, job alert, answer, block). Under the DPDP Act 2023, data about an identifiable person is personal whether or not it is a name.
3. **Internal:** keys, workflow facts and statuses of business processes, ledgers, audit; visible to staff and the owning organisation.
4. **Public:** published content (open jobs, organisation profile, offices) and reference data.

## 2. Masking codes

| Code | Rule |
|---|---|
| M1 | shown in full only to viewers row-level security allows; never in logs, audit details, notification payloads or exports |
| M2 | partial mask outside the owner and staff views (pattern below) |
| M3 | banded or aggregated in analytics and exports (never row-level) |
| M4 | never displayed to anyone; compared as a hash only |
| M5 | displayed as the last characters only |

**Encryption everywhere:** TLS 1.2+ to the database; volume encryption at rest; resume files encrypted by the object store (server-side). Column-level protection is by hashing or tokenising, never reversible encryption, so a database leak reveals no credential or tax identifier.

## 6. Privacy by design (the playbook's "go deeper")

- **Right to erasure:** one function, `candidate_erase()`, in one transaction: profile sections, resumes (files through the outbox), credentials and alerts are deleted; name, contact and salary are blanked; applications, downloads, ledgers and consent evidence stay, pointing at an anonymous profile, because other parties have a legal or contractual right to them. Inactive for 3 years → the scheduler runs the same function.
- **Minimisation:** no card or bank data, no raw national or tax identifier, no message bodies, no password; resume files outside the database; audit details hold column names, never values.
- **Contact details are paid and audited:** an employer reaches email and phone only through a download in the last 30 days, and every read writes an audit event.
- **Test data never holds real personal data:** the generator makes names and uses reserved `.test` domains and generated phone numbers; production data is never copied to development.
- **Open findings:** FND-09 (consent purposes not enforced), FND-10 and FND-11 (erasure leaves answer text and device names), FND-12 (reclassification).
